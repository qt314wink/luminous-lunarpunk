Defiant folklore reassembly through stained glass, kintsugi, and botanical myth

The Fracture

Primordial goddesses shattered into obsidian shards and stained-glass fragments. Each piece carries the weight of forgotten myth — sliced newspaper clippings, photo cutouts, and watercolor bleed painted directly over the collapse.

Kintsugi Repair

Golden veins flow through the cracks. Not to hide the breakage, but to illuminate it. The repair becomes the most luminous part of the whole — glowing kintsugi transforming fracture into sacred geometry.

Botanical Overgrowth

Lush regenerative vines reclaim the repaired surface. Moss, fern, and luminescent fungi thread through mosaic gaps. The goddesses do not return to their original form — they become something wilder, more tactile, more alive.
